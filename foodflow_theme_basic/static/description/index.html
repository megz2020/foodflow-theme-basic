What is FoodFlow?

foodflo.app is the online platform for restaurants: QR menu, online ordering, WhatsApp bot, loyalty, and kitchen sync. Theme Basic gives your Odoo back office the FoodFlow look and links your team to the platform. Full POS styling and live sync need Theme Pro (subscription).
