What is FoodFlow?

foodflo.app is the online platform for restaurants: QR menu, online ordering, WhatsApp bot, loyalty, kitchen display, and reservations. Theme Basic (this free module) styles your Odoo back office. Theme Pro adds POS v2 and live sync (FoodFlow subscription).
